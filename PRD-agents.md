# PRD: Go.dev Agents

Two agents. They live in Slack, in Discord, and on Go.dev Agents.

1. **Competitive Intelligence.** Social signals and competitor intel in one agent.
2. **Ghostwriting.** Posts and replies in the person's voice.

ARC sits on top of both. A result is kept only when it still holds on evidence that was not used to write it. If it fails, it is not sent as ready.

Price later: $10 a month. The check keeps running every week, which is why they pay again.

## Who pays

Reddit agencies, account warmers, Reddit marketers, Reddit ghostwriters, content creators, NSFW creators, and AI content creators.

A founder uses Competitive Intelligence for the whole brand: where it shows up, and whether people are positive or negative. An NSFW creator uses it to watch rival public profiles: karma, where they post, and which posts stay up. Private earnings and DMs are out.

## Competitive Intelligence

One agent. Two jobs.

**Social signals.** Every day, look for people asking for help about the brand, a competitor, or a problem. Skip ads and "I built this" posts. Send the best few to Slack and Discord.

The ARC rule: send a thread only when the reason is a real quote copied from that thread. If the quote is not in the thread, do not send it.

**Competitor intel.** When they ask, answer from Reddit: their brand versus competitors, price, complaints, praise, and comparisons. A founder gets the whole surface and the sentiment. A creator gets public profile movement for rivals they named.

The ARC rule: write the answer from most of the threads. Hold the rest back. Send it as ready only if those held-back threads still agree. At least 6 of 10 decided claims must agree, and no more than one claim may disagree. If there are fewer than 5 threads, send today's answer and do not call it a failure. If the held-back threads never mention the claims, send the answer and do not block it.

One line when the check ran:

> Checked: 4 of 5 claims hold up on threads this answer did not use.

or

> Held back: this answer did not hold up on fresh threads.

The same line goes to Slack and Discord. The quotes sit behind it.

## Ghostwriting

A separate agent. "Write this idea" and "reply to this thread," in their voice.

Build the draft first. Then the same held-back-thread check. Do not block a draft until that check has been watched and is not throwing away good writing. Until then, send the draft and save whether it held up.

## Not in this

The ten safe founder subreddits. Nothing there was taken down. The BanAware line that is already written by hand ("you posted in 3 subreddits today"). A new website screen. A second search just to find more threads. Private earnings.
